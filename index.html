Pocket directory / Amsterdam

Amsterdam.

Amsterdam began around a medieval dam on the Amstel, expanded into a planned 17th-century canal ring, then grew through workers’ districts and across the IJ to an old shipyard now full of art. Those layers are close enough to explore on foot, by bike or ferry, but they feel like different cities. Follow the markets, coffee, records and galleries rather than treating the centre as the whole story.
