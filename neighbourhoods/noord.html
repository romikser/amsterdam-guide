Noord / NDSM.

NDSM’s shipyard closed in 1984; artists and students later reclaimed its vast industrial halls. Ferry over for the cranes, waterfront and large-scale work at STRAAT, but treat Garage Noord as a separate stop, not something beside NDSM.
