Haarlemmerbuurt.

Haarlemmerstraat and Haarlemmerdijk trace the old westward route toward Haarlem, extended as the canal ring grew in the 1600s. The independent shopfronts and cafés make this a better small-street wander than the centre’s busiest retail strips: pair TOKI or Good Beans with a bite on Haarlemmerstraat.
