Festivals & city dates

Two worthwhile public events, not a generic festival calendar.

16–17 Oct / Independent print fair

Bring Your Own Book Fair

Artist books, zines, photography and small-press publishing at Loods6 on KNSM-eiland.

Official event ↗
Timing, caveats & source

Friday 14:00–21:00; Saturday 11:00–21:00. Free entry. Publisher list and programme are on the organiser’s site.

17–18 Oct / City-wide event

Amsterdam Marathon weekend

City Run and 7.5K on Saturday; marathon and half-marathon on Sunday, with a very different street rhythm.

Official event ↗
Timing, caveats & source

Official programme: marathon Sunday 09:00, half-marathon 13:20. Expect route-related travel disruption; consult the organiser’s route and live transit advice. Public event information only.
