Editorial notes

Why these?

These are places to consider, not an algorithmic top-ten. An account can know Amsterdam and still have entirely different taste. We treat Instagram as a way to find leads, then check who made the recommendation, what they actually tried, whether the place still exists at that address, and whether it fits this guide.

Calibrate the source first

For coffee, The Coffeevine’s Amsterdam six explicitly favour bars focused on exceptional coffee rather than hot-food menus; its repeat specialty coverage is useful for coffee, not a blanket vote on art or dinner. It sells coffee subscriptions, so we still check individual venue claims. For casual food, Forking Delicious describes a real burger tasting while saying Le Smash was not a personal favourite: that nuance is more useful than a viral “best” reel. For matcha, posts that discuss tea origin, bitterness, texture and preparation carry more weight than colour or a long queue.

Then verify the place

A venue’s own post can confirm an exhibition, menu or move, but is not an independent quality endorsement. Exact dated Instagram captions and specialist reviews appear under “Hours, bookings & sources” on supported cards; not every listed place has an independently accessible Instagram post. Official venue pages are used for current location, schedule and tickets. Verify same-day hours—especially market stalls and pop-ups.

Useful negatives

We rejected a five-matcha-spots reel that put a Rotterdam tearoom in Amsterdam. We did not route to Suki’s old Elandsgracht address, Rush Hour’s old street number or DAK’s roastery instead of its public showroom. City Records is held while its announced move to Nieuwe Hoogstraat is confirmed. A gallery’s older post or a New York exhibition is not evidence that the same show is on in Amsterdam now.

Photography & maps

Photos are either reusable real-area or venue images with credits and licences, or displayed directly from an official venue page with source attribution. We do not copy Google or Apple Maps customer photos. Apple Maps does not provide a token-free multi-pin embed for this static site; each card has an Apple Maps link, and the home, neighbourhood and category pages offer separate Guide links built from verified current place pins. A few venues with stale or absent Apple Maps listings are omitted from those Guide links and are named in the export notes; complete CSV downloads retain every website pick.
